# Fjordlight Games employee handbook

Fjordlight Games is a fictional 40-person game studio in Umeå. This handbook is test data for the evaluation suite.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00, Monday to Thursday. Fridays have no core hours.
Everyone may work remotely up to three days a week. Full-time remote work needs approval from your team lead.

## Equipment

New employees get a laptop with 32 GB of memory and a second monitor.
The home-office allowance is 6 000 SEK, paid once, for a chair, desk or lamp.
Broken equipment is reported in the #it-help channel.

## Vacation

Employees have 30 days of paid vacation per year.
The studio is closed between Christmas Eve and New Year's Day; those days don't count as vacation.
Vacation requests for summer must be sent before 31 March.

## Release crunch

Fjordlight Games does not do crunch. Overtime is capped at 20 hours per month and is paid at 150 %.

## Learning

Each employee has a learning budget of 12 000 SEK per year for courses, books and conferences.
Two Fridays per quarter are "build days" for personal projects.
